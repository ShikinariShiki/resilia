# RESILIA brand kit

Single source of truth lives in `tokens.json`. No component may hardcode a hex, size, radius, or duration that is not derived from these tokens.

## Feeling

Warm field notebook meets a calm clinic meets a living forest. Grounded and hopeful, never sterile, never cold.

## Color roles

- teal is the action and brand color
- ember is the warm highlight
- amber is reward and coins
- indigo is a rare info accent
- semantic colors only carry state, success, warning, danger, info
- light mode reads like sunlit recycled paper, dark mode reads like warm bark at night
- never shift to slate blue, never use lavender or purple as an accent
- keep text contrast at 4.5 to 1 for body and 3 to 1 for large text in both modes

## Type

- Fraunces for display, h1, h2, editorial credibility for an essay competition
- Hanken Grotesk for body and controls, human and legible
- DM Mono for numbers, XP, HP, timers, coin counts, and HUD labels
- never use Inter or Space Grotesk, never mix more than these three families
- self host through @fontsource so the CSP stays tight and there is no layout shift

## Motion

- vary entrances by content, no single global fade in up blur
- real spring physics from `tokens.motion.spring`, transform and opacity only
- first open intro runs once behind a stored flag
- honor prefers reduced motion with a calm opacity only path, mandatory

## Voice

- calm human mentor, plain words, short lines
- no trailing period on UI copy, no em dash, no semicolon, no banned jargon
- retire the spy theme in Academy, this is a field notebook not a control room
- frame failure as learning, not punishment, keep RPG stakes gentle

## Lia usage

- Lia the axolotl psychologist is the emotional anchor
- ship transparent sprites, never a white box, correct left and right facing variants
- happy, worried, and unsure variants crossfade in time with the line
- keep Lia on the mentor side of the stage, the NPC or the player youth sits opposite

## Do and do not

- do use asymmetric editorial layouts, warm shadows, real icons, bespoke spot art
- do use the grain texture and the warm dark base
- do not use glow orbs, gradient headline text, purple to blue gradients, emoji as bullets or flags
- do not render the whole app in thin single weight outline icons
